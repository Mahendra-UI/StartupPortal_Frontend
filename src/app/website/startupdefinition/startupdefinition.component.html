To streamline the recognition process and reduce the administrative burden on startups operating within the state of Andhra Pradesh, it is hereby informed that the state has formally adopted the definition of a startup as issued by the Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT).

According to DPIIT, a startup is defined as :

  • A company that is incorporated as a Private Limited Company, Partnership firm, or Limited Liability Partnership (llp).
  • It must fulfill the following eligibility criteria:
    • Incorporation Age : The company must be incorporated within the last 10 years.
    • Turnover : The company’s annual turnover must be less than ₹100 crore in any of the previous financial years.
    • Innovation : The company must be working on improving or developing products, services, or processes.
    • Scalability : The company must have a scalable business model capable of creating wealth and jobs.
    • Independence : The company must not be formed as a result of splitting up or restructuring an existing business.

Recognition Process :

Startups registered in the state of Andhra Pradesh with a valid DPIIT Recognition Number are automatically deemed recognized as startups under the State Startup Policy. No additional registration or formalities are required for such startups to be recognized as Startups of Andhra Pradesh.

Additional Support To further ease the burden on startups:

  • Startups with DPIIT Login can directly login to AP Startup One Portal since the Single Sign On (SSO) API Integration with Startup India Portal completed by AP Innovation Society.
  • This step is aimed at reducing duplication and enabling startups to focus on their growth and innovation without administrative hurdles
