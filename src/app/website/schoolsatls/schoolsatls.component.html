To encourage Innovation, Ideation, Creativity and Design Thinking thereby creating a culture of Entrepreneurship in Schools across the state as envisioned in National Innovation and National Education Policy 2020. To facilitate real-time experience and exposure to School Students AP Innovation Society is facilitating with the following activities.

  • Field trips to the nearby Incubation/Acceleration Centres
  • Mentoring the schools with Atal Tinkering Labs (ATL)
  • Hosting School Innovation Challenges every year
  • Promoting the ideas/ innovations/ prototypes generated by students locally, nationally and internationally
  • Develop School Innovation Repository

Currently Andhra Pradesh has more than 482 Schools selected under Atal Innovation Mission wherein AP Innovation Society has played an instrumental role in coordinating the activities both from the NITI Aayog and Government of Andhra Pradesh for facilitating the process
