Mentors work as a kind of fuel for entrepreneurs by sharing their knowledge and contacts harvested through their years of experience

Entrepreneurs begin their startup dreams with a great vision for creating a difference and impact in their thrust areas however the statistics state that 90% of Indian startups don't surpass the five-year threshold of which around 20% of them sink during the first two years of their journey. One of the identified reasons for the failures of these entrepreneurs is inexperience when they start a business. The only way to overcome inexperience quickly and hack their learning curve is through mentoring support.

In the journey of Startups, mentors play a vital role in the success of their business having understood this AP Innovation Society (APIS) is helping the startups incubated with us by connecting and mentoring them with the right mentors who could assist and advise the startups in getting the right support and guidance toward the epitome of success.

Procedure for on-boarding Mentors

The AP Innovation Society recognizes individuals registered with Department for Promotion of Industry and Internal Trade (DPIIT) as Mentors in the state to support and guide startups to grow their business. The framework to onboard mentors and registration of mentor is available at https://maarg.startupindia.gov.in/

Are you a Startup looking for Mentorship Support

Mentorship Process

  • After Successful registration, the Startup can click on the” Connect with Mentor” tab on the portal.
  • You can select any mentor either from the pool of mentors available in our portal.
  • After submitting the connection request, the mentor shall evaluate your application.
  • A Startup may connect with multiple mentors depending on the availability of the Mentors.
